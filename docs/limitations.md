# Limits of this assessment edition

The completed outcome reports concern synthetic records and bounded software
experiments. They do not establish a complete deployed GBI-DCSE product.

![Assessment update](application/figures/updated.svg)

- Dataset integrity is not evidence of IHS representativeness.
- A successful inference process may still yield no admissible answer.
- The added eight-case run is development evidence, not a held-out comparison.
- A schema-valid deterministic resource is not an LLM success or a clinical fact.
- The original v3 erratum and three out-of-scope claims remain unresolved in their
  original interpretation; they are not counted as clinical successes.
- The included selected summaries do not expose the raw data or complete source
  needed for independent reproduction of the underlying experiments.
- No new empirical trial was run while assembling this edition. Local checks
  validate publication consistency only.
- No professional, institutional, regulatory or intellectual-property clearance
  is conferred by a software PASS.

Public sharing requires an owner decision about the precise content. Review-only
packaging is not a finding about the current status of any patent or trade secret.
